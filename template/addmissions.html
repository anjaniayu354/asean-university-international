02 — International Programs

Mandatory Identity Documents

Admission criteria, expanded academic prerequisites, and student visa pathways for ASEAN Universities.

01

National ID Card

Mainly utilized during the initial account creation on university portals to validate nationality and home country background.

  • High-resolution color scan, fully legible, with zero cropped margins
  • Must be officially translated into English by a Certified Sworn Translator if the original format is non-bilingual
02

International Passport

The most critical legal document required for issuing the Official Letter of Acceptance (LoA) and processing visa sponsorship.

  • Validity Policy: Minimum of 6 to 18 months remaining validity prior to entry
  • Specific destination authorities (e.g., Malaysia's EMGS) mandate a comprehensive scan of every single page, including blank ones
03 — Visa Pathway

Step-by-Step Student Visa Process

Comprehensive guide to admission and immigration — five phases from offer letter to student pass endorsement.

1

Receiving Official LoA & Sponsorship Approval

Upon successfully clearing admissions and securing your first-semester tuition payment, the university dispatches your Unconditional LoA alongside official Visa Approval Letters (VAL) or legal institutional sponsorships.

2

Visa Application Submission

Depending on the host country, you either finalize a direct cloud-based digital process (e.g., Singapore's ICA e-Service) or submit physical files to the corresponding Embassy/Consulate General in your home nation to obtain a Single Entry Visa (SEV).

3

Compulsory Medical Surveillance

Undergoing rigorous diagnostic tests (blood testing, chest X-rays) at accredited panel clinics pre-departure. Many regions require a secondary post-arrival routing check at the university medical center within 7 days of landing.

4

Border Immigration Clearance

Present yourself at designated Special International Student counters at the arrival airport. Ready your physical Passport, LoA, VAL/IPA documents, and return ticket itinerary to secure a temporary entry endorsement stamp.

5

Student Pass Conversion & Endorsement

Surrender your passport to the university's International Student Office. They coordinate directly with domestic Immigration Headquarters to secure a multi-entry Student Pass sticker/card, typically valid for 1 year and renewable annually.

💡

Critical Solvency Note Regarding Visa Clearances

Immigration departments across the ASEAN zone uniformly demand verified proof of liquidity via a formal Bank Statement. This security fund must comfortably cover tuition fees plus cost of living overheads for at least 1 full calendar academic year. Ensure these funds are deposited and matured well before launching formal visa applications.
