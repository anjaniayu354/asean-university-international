Frequently Asked Questions

Can I study the Master programme from my own country?

Yes. Every programme AUI offers can be studied from anywhere in the world — choose to study online, or at your own pace with or without attending classes.

Is the education accredited if I study online?

Whichever study method you choose, you'll experience the same high‑quality, accredited education programme of study.

Are scholarships available for the Master programme?

Yes. Scholarship is available for the Master programme (and also for Bachelor and Doctor). Contact admissions at admin@aui.university for the current special offer.

How large is the AUI student community?

AUI has nearly 30,000 students and 20,000 alumni, with 3,000+ students enrolled from 5+ countries, 5 academic faculties, 20+ research centers, and 100+ industry partnerships worldwide.

How do I contact admissions?

Email admin@aui.university, call +60 165 680 906, or use the student portal (portal-aui). Our office is at F-2-23, Jalan BG 3B/1 Bangi Gateway 3B, Seksyen 15, 43650 Bandar Baru Bangi, Selangor, Malaysia.
