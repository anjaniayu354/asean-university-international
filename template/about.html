02 — Our Journey

From Yales to ASEAN University International

Perjalanan lebih dari dua dekade — dari lembaga pendidikan kecil hingga universitas internasional dengan jaringan global.

2001 – 2002
Foundation

Yales, Board of Education Management

Yales, Board of Education Management was established, running a few courses such as Management, Business Law, and TOEFL under the supervision of Dr. Suhendar.

2003 – 2004
Collaboration

Expanded Collaboration with Singapore

Expanded collaboration with Explomo Technical Services (Singapore), promoting Corporate Law and International Hospitality with "Akademi Perhotelan Kartika".

2005 – 2010
Growth

Akademi Yayasan Taruma Negara

Yales Foundation expanded into "Akademi Yayasan Taruma Negara", offering Executive Diplomas in Management and Secretarial studies.

2011
Milestone

ASEAN University Officially Founded

ASEAN University was officially founded in Labuan, Malaysia, as a significant milestone in regional education standards.

2019 – Present
Global Expansion

Rebranded as ASEAN University International

Rebranded as ASEAN University International in Kuala Lumpur. Partnering with global institutions like Al-Khalifa Business School and ALFA University College.
